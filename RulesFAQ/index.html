THE TEN RULES

How we play together

Keep the kingdom strong, make room for every alliance, and give each other a reason to stay.

01
NAP5

Keep cities peaceful

No city attacks against NAP5 members, including farms and anyone you find mildly annoying.

Attacking PHX is mandatory. Peace has its limits.

02
CASTLE & KVK

Fight as one kingdom

Follow the battle plan, support rally leaders, and join your assigned groups.

Soloing for points is not a battle strategy.

03
CASTLE ROTATION

Share the crown

The castle rotates between alliances. Each alliance chooses its own King or Queen.

Flame Dragon Tyrant and transfer periods may need adjustments agreed through kingdom leadership.

04
KVK PREP

Prepare before you spend

Save resources, speedups, and upgrades for KvK Prep wherever possible. Kingdom success comes before individual rankings.

Winning together beats flexing a shiny SG avatar frame.

05
ALL-OUT EVENT

Gather tiles. No city attacks.

We’re collecting resources, not grudges. All-Out ends after Castle Battle so everyone can prepare for the next Strongest Governor day.

Gathering attacks are suspended during the Flame Dragon event. Yes, even StreetDoc gets a break.

06
RESPECT

Banter, not bullying

Banter is welcome. Bullying, harassment, threats, and personal attacks are not.

Be funny, not the reason someone transfers.

07
WORLD CHAT

Keep it fun and civil-ish

World Chat is not a courtroom or the season finale of your favourite drama series.

Inform leadership before launching a crusade.

08
SANCTUARIES & REWARDS

Share rewards fairly

Sanctuary rewards are distributed fairly among all alliances. No one alliance takes priority.

Rally leads get all buff priority.

09
OUTPOSTS

Allocate as fairly as possible

There aren’t enough outposts for everyone, so some alliances may miss out.

Jump to the necessary alliance if you need a buff.

10
NEWCOMERS

Send the welcome march

On arrival, every newcomer sends a one-troop march to [TNW] Dirtybob. Consider it the official kingdom handshake.

He will judge you by your troop birth.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Can I attack a city during All-Out?

No. All-Out is for gathering tiles only; city attacks are off the table. It ends after Castle Battle so everyone can prepare for Strongest Governor.

Who can I attack under NAP5?

Do not attack NAP5 members’ cities, including farms. The poster’s PHX line is the kingdom’s one very specific exception.

How does castle rotation work?

The castle rotates between alliances, and each alliance chooses its own King or Queen. Any changes for Flame Dragon Tyrant or transfer periods should be agreed through kingdom leadership.

How should I help during Castle or KvK?

Follow the battle plan, support rally leaders, and join the group you were assigned to. Coordinate with leadership if you need clarification.

How are sanctuary rewards and buffs handled?

Sanctuary rewards are shared fairly among alliances. Rally leads receive priority for all buffs.

What should a newcomer do?

Send a one-troop march to [TNW] Dirtybob when you arrive. For alliance questions, browse the directory or contact kingdom leadership.
